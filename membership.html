Membership

Join the club and play all year round

Why Join?

We have membership for every age. All members can book courts, play in club leagues and get cheaper coaching.

Membership runs from 1 April to 31 March. You can join at any time of the year.

Prices

Type Price per year
Adult 220 euro
Student (18-23) 110 euro
Junior (under 18) 80 euro
Family 450 euro
Senior (65+) 130 euro

Equipment

You can find rackets and balls through Tennis Ireland. Beginners can borrow club rackets for free. To join, contact us.
