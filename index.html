About the Club

Nore Valley Tennis Club is a friendly club beside the River Nore in Kilkenny. Everyone is welcome, from complete beginners to experienced players.

We run coaching for juniors and adults, social tennis every week and club leagues all year round. Our floodlit courts mean you can play in the evenings too.

Read about our history and facilities, see our membership prices, or contact us to book a free trial session.
