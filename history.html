History and Facilities

More than 60 years of tennis by the River Nore

Our History

The club was started in 1962 by a few local families. They marked out two grass courts in a field beside the river.

In 1995 the grass courts were replaced with all-weather courts. In 2010 we opened a new clubhouse, paid for by members' fundraising. Today the club has over 300 members.

Club Facilities

  • 6 all-weather courts (4 with floodlights)
  • Clubhouse with changing rooms and showers
  • Cafe and social area
  • Practice wall and ball machine
  • Free parking
